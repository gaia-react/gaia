# Chromatic Opt-Out

Ask Claude to walk through the opt-out steps. The procedure involves uninstalling `chromatic`, deleting the GitHub workflow and `frontend/.storybook/chromatic/` folder, and updating `frontend/.storybook/preview.ts` to use `WrapDecorator` directly.

See [[Chromatic]] for the full removal flow.
